How the model was built. Parcel outlines are traced from the parcel-division plan (p.18) and scaled so the 22 traced areas match the published site areas; each matches to within 8%, most within 3%. Heights, floor counts, typical floor plates, coverage and podium floors come from the unit tables (pp.11, 13, 15). Podiums are each parcel shrunk about its centre to the published coverage area. Towers are square plates of the published typical floor area, placed by eye from the master plan (p.6). Setback lines are offset per side from the control tables (pp.19–21). Basement depth = published basement area ÷ parcel area, using B1 5.5 m, B2 4.0 m, B3/B4 3.8 m (p.8). The loop road, entrances, station and walking routes are traced approximately from pp.22, 54, 57, 66–72. Program colours follow the p.28 axonometric, and some tower assignments there are approximate. The ground is shown flat; real grades run from about 66 m to 76 m. Use this for orientation and coordination, not as survey.

Flags for the competition team

Where this 2024 guideline meets, or conflicts with, the competition package.

02-03 + 02-04 is the competition site. The Twin Towers landmark (key 6 on p.6, key 2 on p.15) sits on parcels 02-03 and 02-04, south of 02-01/02-02 and on Shangcheng Ave. Together they are 30,843 m² here, against 29,778.53 m² in the competition red line. 02-01 + 02-02 match the “Plot 1 (north)” package. This mapping is inferred from position and area; the guideline itself never says “Plot 2”.
Heights: 180 m and 172 m here, ≤ 200 m in the planning conditions. This guideline (Nov 2024) suggests 180 m for 02-03 and 172 m for 02-04. The later planning conditions (义规条件[2025]428号, 17 Nov 2025) allow ≤ 200 m. The airport ceiling of 264 m elevation caps the highest point at about 188–192 m above grade. The planning conditions govern. Treat 180/172 as the city’s design intent, not a cap.
FAR lines up. The guideline’s above-ground GFA for 02-03 + 02-04 is 157,920 m². On the competition net site of 23,174.77 m² that is FAR 6.81, inside the 6.0–7.0 band. The guideline’s own FARs (4.8 and 5.4) use the gross parcel areas, including the green that is handed to the city.
Hotel on the twin tower. The program axonometric (p.28) puts a five-star hotel at the top of the tallest twin tower. The planning conditions say a hotel, if built, must be ≥ 5,000 m² and held, not sold.
Parking ratio units are not printed. Pages 22 and 65 give ratios of 0.70 (02-03) and 0.90 (02-04) without units. The planning conditions require 0.6 spaces per 100 m² of above-ground area. If the guideline means the same unit, 02-04 is planned well above the minimum: about 727 spaces on 80,782 m².
Ratio mismatch for 01-03. Page 22 gives 0.70; page 65 gives 0.60. Every other parcel matches.
Vehicle access to the twin towers. The control table (p.21) puts the main vehicle access at the SE corner of 02-03 and the SW corner of 02-04, i.e. where the two meet near Shangcheng Ave. Check this against the no-access frontage bars on red-line drawing 01 before you place ramps.
Underground detail covers Unit 01 only. The loop, ramps, fire compartments and B1–B4 layouts (pp.66–77) are drawn for Unit 01. Unit 02 gets basement areas, ratios and an “encouraged to connect to the intercity-rail underground space” note (p.22). The B3 tunnel under Shangcheng Ave (from 01-11) is the one stated link across the avenue.
“5万” parking zones. Pages 74–76 limit basement parking zones to “不大于5万” without a unit. Read as 50,000 m² under the Zhejiang guideline; confirm with the fire consultant.
Duplicate page. Page 73 repeats page 23’s underground table.
