How the auto-adjust works. The tower you last touched leads. If total FAR GFA falls below FAR 6.0, the other tower first adds floors up to the height cap, then widens its floor plate (up to 55 m per side and the tower control line). If the total goes over FAR 7.0, it drops floors (or shrinks its floor plate first, per the toggle) down to 100 m, the brief's “super-high-rise” threshold, and the H/7 slenderness minimum. Modelling assumptions: office floors start above the podium; refuge floors (one every ≤ 50 m on towers over 100 m, per GB 50016) are excluded from FAR, which matches the brief's program (46,000 + 116,000 = 162,000 m² counted, plus 8,000 m² refuge); the podium footprint is the podium control line inset to podium GFA ÷ floors. Site geometry is traced from the 2000-system coordinates on drawing 01 and reproduces the documented areas to within 0.3%. The east green strip width is solved so the net site equals 23,174.77 m². Treat this as a massing check, not a survey.

Corrections & flags

Things in the folder that a reader without Chinese would miss.

Green-building rating was corrected to Three-Star. Document 03 prints “Two-Star” (二星级), but a red annotation on the scan says it is wrong and was later confirmed as Three-Star (有误，后确认为三星级). The brief also requires Three-Star for the towers.
Civil-defense GFA figures in 04 are wrong. Document 04 gives 178,671–208,450 m² (FAR 6–7 applied to the full 29,778.53 m² site). The annotation says to deduct the planned green first (规模数据有误，应扣除规划绿地后再计算计容面积), giving 139,049–162,223 m². This tool uses the corrected basis.
The two tender .doc files have identical text. 义乌双子塔项目设计招标文件终稿.doc and …终稿(2).doc are the same size and produce identical text; only file metadata differs. Use either one.
There is no item 05. The red-line folder numbering jumps from 04 to 06. Planning conditions 02 lists three attachments (01, 03, 04), and all are present.
The airport limit sets the real height cap. Airport clearance is an absolute 264 m elevation (Yellow Sea datum), and it applies to the highest point, including tower cranes during construction. Grade is about 72.4–75.9 m, so the practical ceiling is about 188–192 m above grade, below the 200 m planning limit.
Validity periods. The planning conditions (02) are valid for two years from 17 Nov 2025. The construction conditions (03) must be reissued if the land is not sold within one year of 3 Dec 2025. The brief gives the bid deadline as 10 Sep 2026, 09:00 Beijing time.
