Yiwu Elevation Growth Lab · how it works

The lab grows figures from the Yiwu atlas on every face of the two towers and on the plinth's plans. It turns those figures into massing: the towers are carved with soft scoops, and the plinth is built up floor by floor from pairs of plans. Then it cuts every floor plan and checks egress and area. This sheet follows one build with your base settings (Plinthv2). It shows the key steps and the mechanism behind each, with your values in red.

Masses from the Podium Lab

The two towers and the plinth are read from the Podium Lab's saved state, or from the control lines if there is none. Each mass gets its own local axes (a, b) along its rotation.

  • Towers: each 46 × 46 m tower footprint is scaled along its own axes by 0.70 × 1.15, giving 32.2 × 52.9 m. They rise 180 m: a 6 m ground floor, then 4.2 m floors, 43 floors in all.
  • Plinth: with Floors down at 0 it keeps its full 24 m: five storeys at 0, 5.5, 10, 14.5 and 19 m (5.5 m ground, 4.5 m above). A 4.5 m basement sits under its ground-floor footprint.

Faces: elevations and plans

TEach tower is unrolled into four elevations: S and N are 32.2 × 180 m, E and W are 52.9 × 180 m. Each is a plane (u along the face, v = height).

PThe plinth gets 6 plans, all cut to its real footprint (19,483 m², not the 23,735 m² bounding rectangle):

  • the basement plan makes B1 alone;
  • plans 1–5 make the five floors above ground, as cyclic pairs: G = 1+2, 1 = 2+3, 2 = 3+4, 3 = 4+5, and the top floor 4 = 5+1;
  • the default plan is not grown: it only holds the shared paint (step 3).

Density field and paint

Every face carries a density field D(u, v) from 0 to 1. With these settings:

  • Ta horizontal ramp across each elevation, D = 0.95 → 0.20, with midpoint 0.5, sharpness 0.3, and ± 0.06 noise at 24 m;
  • Pno gradient (None · paint only): D = 0 plus ± 0.26 noise at 12 m, plus paint. Painting the default plan replaces every plan's own paint, so one painting sets all plinth floors.

D drives three things:

  • a seed survives with probability D at its centre;
  • figures shrink by up to 60% × 0.85 (towers) or × 0.62 (plinth) where D = 1;
  • growth splits edges in proportion to 0.35 + 0.65·D.

4

Seeds from the atlas

Figures are drawn at random from the atlas's 107 single footprints (board B). Each face's 80-gene genome picks:

  • the figure, its position (u, v) and a quarter turn;
  • a jitter of ± 46° on the towers (0° on the plinth);
  • a scale of 0.6–2.0 on the towers (0.7–1.65 on the plinth).

Each figure's area = cover × face area / count × Size ×. A seed is rejected if it overlaps another, pushes the face over its cover, or (plinth) leaves the footprint. Every rejection shrinks the next seed by 3% until the count is met.

5

Differential growth + agents

Each figure outline is a ring of nodes, run for 240 steps. Every step:

  • springs pull each edge toward 1.2 m;
  • smoothing and bending act on the ring;
  • figures repel within 2.0 m of each other, and folds of one outline within 1.8 m;
  • edges split with p = min(0.05, field · gain · 4 / Σw), only where they are longer than 0.72 m and not touching another figure;
  • each face is capped at 4,000 nodes.

Agents: every 3 steps, until 70% of the run, the 34% unhappiest figures try four gaussian hops (σ = 4 m × (0.25 + T)) and two quarter turns. Happiness weighs density 1, crossing 1 (target 35%), support 0.6 and neighbours 0.4. T anneals from 0.45 to 0.

6

Straighten the figures

Each grown outline is regularised before massing, the same way for both groups:

  • simplify: Douglas–Peucker at 6 m tolerance;
  • square: every run is snapped horizontal or vertical to the face, parallel runs merge, and runs under 2.5 m are dropped;
  • snap T: on the towers, each figure's bottom and top edges move to the nearest floor line (6 m, then every 4.2 m).

Plinth plans are not snapped; they are smoothed later, in step 8.

TTowers: soft scoops carved per storey

Each tower starts solid. On each face, the figures of at least 23 m² are taken largest first, up to 24 per face and 61% of the face's area. Each one:

  • is rasterised at 0.5 m;
  • is opened with a disc of radius min(6.25 m, 0.85 × its own half-thickness), so no figure is lost;
  • joins the others, and the union is closed at 6.25 m;
  • becomes a signed distance s(u, v) to that outline, > 0 inside.

Then every storey is cut at mid-height, on a 0.25 m grid. A point at depth w behind face F is void if s > 0 and w < D·min(1, s / 9.4 m)0.9. Here D = min(9.5 m, distance to the core − 3 m), so a scoop is 9.5 m deep where the outline is wide and shallow near its edges. The 10 × 10 m core is always solid.

Each storey's outline is traced (marching squares), simplified to 6 cm and extruded. Pieces under 4 m² are removed.

PPlinth: mass from the figures, floor by floor

For each plan, the figures of at least 11 m² are rasterised at 0.5 m and processed in four steps:

  • round: opened by up to 2 m, never more than 0.85 × their half-thickness;
  • smooth: blurred over 3 m and cut at ½, which removes the 2.5 m stair-steps of the squared edges;
  • merge: closed at 3 m, so figures closer than about 6 m join;
  • distance: turned into a signed distance splan.

Each floor is then built from its plans:

  • floors above ground: union of the floor's two plans, s = max(sk, sk+1), clipped to the plinth's outline at that storey;
  • coverage: left as grown (a target would offset s evenly);
  • pieces: those under 40 m² are dropped, and all others kept;
  • basement: the basement plan's figures are dug out of a 4.5 m slab of earth, with a 1 m earth floor under them.

Each floor is traced and extruded on its own, so the plinth terraces wherever its pairs differ.

Cores, floor plans, egress and area

Every floor is cut 1.2 m above its level and rasterised at 0.5 m. Ways out:

  • towers: the 10 × 10 m core;
  • plinth: any tower core reaching into it, plus plinth cores (0 in your settings);
  • ground floors: any floor cell on the outside wall.

A breadth-first search from those exits gives every cell its distance. Each separate part of at least 4 m² that can't reach an exit is joined to one by the cheapest straight-edged route:

  • cost: 1,000 per new cell (2,000 outside the plinth), 1 per turn, 0 over existing floor;
  • width: max(3 m, occupants × 5 mm, 1.1 m), where occupants = area / 9.3 m²;
  • form: a 0.3 m plate.

Finally, floors over 37.5 m of travel are flagged, and the GFA above ground is checked against 46,000 m².

Outputs and saving

3D: one closed mesh per tower and for the plinth, plus the basement earth and any bridge plates.

OBJ: masses, earth, bridges and every grown curve, each on its own layer. Metres, Z up, Podium Lab coordinates.

Saved settings: a save holds every setting (both groups), the genomes, all paint (each face's and the default plan's), and the view. A file is applied over the defaults, so anything it lacks takes its default value. Reset to base settings returns to these values (Plinthv2).
